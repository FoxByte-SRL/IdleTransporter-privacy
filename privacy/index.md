# Idle Transporter Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 15, 2026  
**Last updated:** October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FoxByte SRL ("FoxByte," "we," "us," or
"our") processes information when you play **Idle Transporter**, including
the public demo.

## 1. Who is responsible for the data?

FoxByte SRL is the data controller for the optional gameplay analytics
described in this policy.

**Registered address:** Str. Veseliei nr. 1A, Dej, Cluj, Romania  
**Privacy contact:** [info@foxbyte.ro](mailto:info@foxbyte.ro)

## 2. Optional gameplay analytics

Idle Transporter asks for your permission before enabling gameplay analytics.
Analytics is optional, is off until you select **Allow Analytics**, and is not
required to play the game. Choosing **No Thanks** does not limit any game
feature.

If you allow analytics, we use Unity Analytics to process:

- gameplay progress and milestones;
- virtual vehicle purchases and sales;
- upgrade choices and levels;
- vehicle breakdown and repair outcomes;
- cargo-crate outcomes;
- session length and general interaction counts;
- game and analytics SDK versions;
- platform, operating-system, device, and application information made
  available by the Unity Analytics SDK;
- approximate country or region;
- IP address during transmission; and
- a random installation-specific identifier, analytics user identifier,
  session identifier, event identifier, and event timestamp.

We use this information to understand whether players can follow the tutorial,
where demo progression becomes unclear or unbalanced, how game systems are
used, and whether technical or performance problems affect play.

We do **not** intentionally send your name, email address, Steam ID, local save
file, computer username, free-form text, or information about activity outside
Idle Transporter through gameplay analytics.

## 3. Legal basis

We process optional gameplay analytics on the basis of your consent. You can
refuse consent or withdraw it at any time without affecting your ability to
play. Withdrawal does not make processing that occurred before withdrawal
unlawful.

## 4. Unity Analytics

Unity Technologies provides Unity Analytics and processes analytics data on
our behalf. Unity may process data in countries outside your country of
residence and uses contractual and organizational safeguards for applicable
international transfers.

You can read Unity's game-player privacy information here:
[Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy).

## 5. Steam links and campaign reporting

Idle Transporter can open its Steam store page when you choose a Steam or
wishlist button. The link can contain a non-personal campaign label identifying
that it came from the demo. Steam provides FoxByte with aggregated visit and
conversion statistics and does not provide us with Steam IDs through its UTM
Analytics report. Steam processes activity on its platform under its own
privacy terms.

## 6. Local game data

Game progress, settings, and your analytics choice are stored locally on your
device. Idle Transporter's gameplay analytics does not upload your save file.
Removing the game or clearing its local data can remove these local records.

## 7. Sharing and sale

We do not sell gameplay analytics or use it for targeted advertising. We share
analytics information only with service providers that process it for the
purposes described here, such as Unity Technologies, or when disclosure is
required by law.

## 8. Retention

Unity Analytics retains personal data for up to 13 months by default. We keep
exported identifiable analytics only for as long as reasonably necessary to
analyze and improve the relevant demo or release. Aggregated statistics that
no longer identify an installation may be retained for longer.

## 9. Your choices and rights

In Idle Transporter, open **Settings → Privacy** to:

- turn optional gameplay analytics on or off;
- open this Privacy Policy; or
- request deletion of analytics data associated with this installation.

Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to request access,
correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection, and to complain
to your local data-protection authority. To exercise a right, contact us at
[info@foxbyte.ro](mailto:info@foxbyte.ro). We may need enough information to
verify and locate the relevant analytics installation. Please do not send
passwords or sensitive personal information.

## 10. Children

Idle Transporter is not directed to children under 13. If local law requires a
parent or guardian to authorize analytics consent, do not enable analytics
without that authorization. A parent or guardian who believes a child has
provided personal data can contact us to request deletion.

## 11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect
analytics information. No online service or transmission method can be
guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Idle Transporter and its service providers change.
We will update the date above and, when a change materially affects consent,
ask for a new choice before enabling the changed analytics processing.
